#### 4.1.2 Strength Training

**Training Phases:**

**Phase 1: Anatomical Adaptation (Weeks 1-4)**
- Goal: Build work capacity, Teach proper technique
- Intensity: 50-60% 1RM
- Volume: 3 sets × 12-15 reps
- Exercises: Multi-joint, Full body

**Phase 2: Hypertrophy (Weeks 5-8)**
- Goal: Increase muscle mass
- Intensity: 70-80% 1RM
- Volume: 3-4 sets × 8-12 reps
- Exercises: Compound and accessory lifts

**Phase 3: Maximal Strength (Weeks 9-12)**
- Goal: Increase maximal force production
- Intensity: 85-95% 1RM
- Volume: 3-5 sets × 3-6 reps
- Exercises: Core lifts (squat, deadlift, bench press)

**Phase 4: Power (Weeks 13-16)**
- Goal: Convert strength to sport-specific power
- Intensity: 30-60% 1RM (explosive) or bodyweight
- Volume: 3-5 sets × 3-5 reps
- Exercises: Olympic lifts, Plyometrics, Medicine ball throws

**Example Strength Workout (Maximal Strength Phase):**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Intensity | Rest |
|----------|------------|-----------|------|
| Back Squat | 5 × 5 | 85% 1RM | 3 min |
| Romanian Deadlift | 4 × 6 | 75% 1RM | 2 min |
| Bench Press | 4 × 6 | 80% 1RM | 2.5 min |
| Pull-ups (weighted) | 3 × 6 | [Body weight + X kg] | 2 min |
| Core stability (plank variations) | 3 × 30-60s | Bodyweight | 1 min |
