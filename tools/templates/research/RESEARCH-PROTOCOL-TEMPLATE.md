## 13. Risks and Contingency Plans

### 13.1 Recruitment Challenges
**Risk:** Difficulty recruiting sufficient participants
**Contingency:**
- Expand recruitment sites
- Extend recruitment period
- Modify inclusion/exclusion criteria (with IRB approval)

### 13.2 Data Quality Issues
**Risk:** High rate of missing data
**Contingency:**
- Implement real-time data quality checks
- Increase follow-up efforts
- Use advanced imputation methods

### 13.3 Resource Constraints
**Risk:** Budget or personnel limitations
**Contingency:**
- Prioritize primary outcomes
- Seek additional funding
- Collaborate with other institutions
