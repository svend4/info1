## 2. GUEST EXPERIENCE JOURNEY

### 2.1 Pre-Arrival

**Touchpoints:**
1. Website/Online booking
2. Reservation confirmation
3. Pre-arrival communication

**Standards:**

**Website Experience:**
- Fast loading times (<3 seconds)
- Mobile-responsive design
- Clear information on amenities, rates, policies
- Easy booking process (≤3 clicks to reserve)
- High-quality photos and virtual tours

**Reservation Confirmation:**
- Immediate email confirmation upon booking
- All details accurate (dates, room type, rate, special requests)
- Personalized message from General Manager (for luxury properties)
- Contact information for questions

**Pre-Arrival Communication:**
- **Timing:** 24-48 hours before arrival
- **Content:**
  - Welcome message
  - Weather forecast for arrival day
  - Suggested activities and dining options
  - Check-in time and directions
  - Offer to arrange transportation or pre-arrival amenities
- **For VIPs/Returning Guests:** Reference past preferences and stays

**Special Occasions:**
- Proactively identify special occasions (birthdays, anniversaries) from reservation notes
- Prepare surprise amenity or room decoration

### 2.2 Arrival

**Touchpoints:**
1. Transportation/Parking
2. Entrance/Lobby
3. Front Desk Check-In

**Standards:**

**Arrival Transport (if applicable):**
- Driver greets guest by name
- Clean, well-maintained vehicle
- Driver assistance with luggage
- Pleasant conversation (without being intrusive)

**Entrance:**
- Doorman greets within [10 seconds] of vehicle arrival
- Warm greeting: "Good [morning/afternoon/evening], welcome to [Property Name]!"
- Open door, assist with luggage
- Direct guest to reception desk
- **Timing Standard:** Guest acknowledged within [30 seconds] of entering lobby

**Front Desk Check-In:**

**Standard Process (5-Star):**
1. **Greeting (15 seconds):**
   - Stand, smile, make eye contact
   - "Good [time of day], Mr./Ms. [Name], welcome to [Property Name]. We've been expecting you."

2. **Verification (30 seconds):**
   - Confirm reservation details discreetly
   - "I see you're with us for [X] nights in a [room type]. Is that correct?"

3. **Upsell Opportunity (if applicable, 30 seconds):**
   - "I notice we have a [room upgrade] available. Would you be interested for an additional $[X] per night?"

4. **Registration (1 minute):**
   - Minimize paperwork (pre-fill forms where possible)
   - Use tablet/digital check-in for efficiency
   - Brief explanation of signature requirements

5. **Information Delivery (1-2 minutes):**
   - Explain key amenities: Breakfast time/location, Spa, Fitness center, Wi-Fi code
   - Highlight unique features
   - "Your room is on the [Xth floor]. Let me show you on this map."

6. **Key Handoff:**
   - Present room keys in folder with map and amenity information
   - "You're in room [number]. Your luggage will arrive shortly."

7. **Escort to Room (Luxury standard):**
   - "May I escort you to your room?"
   - Orient guest during walk: Point out facilities, Operate room features
   - Room presentation at door

**Check-In Time Standard:**
- Total process: [3-5 minutes for standard, 7-10 minutes with upgrade discussion]
- Wait time: [No more than 3 minutes if queue exists]

**VIP/Loyalty Member Check-In:**
- Acknowledge status immediately: "Welcome back, Mr./Ms. [Name]. It's a pleasure to see you again."
- Check-in from lounge or in-room (no front desk)
- Personalized welcome amenity in room
- Reference past preferences proactively

### 2.3 In-Stay Experience

**Touchpoints:**
1. Room experience
2. Dining
3. Concierge services
4. Housekeeping
5. Amenities (Spa, Fitness, Pool)
6. Guest interactions throughout property

**Room Standards:**

**Upon Entry:**
- Room temperature: [21-22°C / 70-72°F]
- Lighting: Appropriate for time of day (dim for evening arrival)
- Curtains: [Drawn for evening, Open for day]
- Amenities fully stocked:
  - [X] towels per guest (bath, hand, face, bath mat)
  - Toiletries: Shampoo, Conditioner, Body wash, Lotion, Soap
  - Coffee/tea station fully stocked
  - [X] bottles of complimentary water
  - Robe and slippers (for upscale properties)
  - Ice bucket with fresh ice (luxury properties)

**Turn-Down Service (Luxury/Upscale):**
- **Timing:** Between [6:00-8:30 PM]
- **Procedure:**
  - Lights dimmed
  - Bed covers turned down at 45-degree angle
  - Pillow placed on bed
  - Chocolate or amenity on pillow
  - Curtains closed
  - Bathroom tidied (towels replaced, toiletries restocked)
  - Next day's weather forecast card placed on nightstand

**Dining Standards:**

**Restaurant Seating:**
- Guests greeted within [1 minute] of entering
- Escorted to table (not pointed)
- Chair pulled out for guests
- Menus presented with description of specials

**Service Timing:**
- Drinks delivered within [5 minutes] of order
- Appetizers within [10 minutes]
- Entrees within [20 minutes]
- Check delivered within [2 minutes] of request

**Table Maintenance:**
- Water glasses never less than half full
- Used plates cleared within [2 minutes] of guest finishing
- Crumbs swept between courses

**Concierge Standards:**
- Answer guest inquiries within [1 minute] in person, [2 hours] for email requests
- Provide at least [3] options for any recommendation request
- Secure reservations/tickets for guests (track until confirmed)
- Handwritten thank-you notes for significant requests
- Follow up on next day: "How was [restaurant/show] last night?"

**Housekeeping Standards:**

**Daily Service:**
- Timing: Between [10:00 AM - 4:00 PM] unless guest indicates preference
- Knock and announce: "Housekeeping!" Wait [10 seconds] for response
- If "Do Not Disturb" sign: Check back every [2 hours], maximum respect guest privacy
- Service duration: [30 minutes] for standard room

**Room Cleaning Checklist:**
- [ ] Bed made (hospital corners, pillows fluffed and arranged)
- [ ] All surfaces dusted and wiped
- [ ] Floors vacuumed/mopped
- [ ] Bathroom scrubbed (toilet, sink, shower/tub, mirrors spotless)
- [ ] Towels replaced if used (hanging towels replaced if policy or if on floor)
- [ ] Toiletries restocked
- [ ] Trash emptied
- [ ] Glassware replaced or cleaned
- [ ] Coffee/tea station restocked
- [ ] Final quality check before leaving

**Special Touches:**
- Guest belongings neatly arranged (do not move personal items excessively)
- TV remote placed on bedside table
- Promotional materials or event flyers placed thoughtfully

### 2.4 Check-Out and Departure

**Touchpoints:**
1. Check-out process
2. Farewell
3. Post-stay communication

**Check-Out Standards:**

**Express Check-Out:**
- Option to check out via TV, Mobile app, or drop envelope in designated box
- Bill slipped under door by [6:00 AM] on departure day

**Front Desk Check-Out:**
- Greeting: "Good [time of day], Mr./Ms. [Name]. Were you checking out today?"
- Inquiry: "How was your stay? Is there anything we could have done better?"
- Review charges, offer printed receipt
- Offer assistance with luggage or transportation
- Farewell: "Thank you for staying with us. We hope to welcome you back soon."
- **Time Standard:** [2-3 minutes]

**Departure:**
- Doorman assists with luggage loading
- Warm farewell: "Goodbye, Mr./Ms. [Name]. Have a wonderful [day/trip]!"
- Wave as guest departs

**Post-Stay:**
- Survey email sent [24 hours] after check-out
- Personalized thank-you email from GM (for stays >3 nights or VIPs)
- Respond to online reviews within [48 hours]
- Address any complaints or issues mentioned in surveys within [24 hours]

**Appearance:**

**Technical Standards:**

- Process check-in/out in [3-5 minutes]
- Handle reservation changes accurately
- Up-sell rate: [Target X% of check-ins]

**Problem Resolution:**
- Listen actively without interrupting
- Apologize sincerely (even if not directly at fault)
- Take ownership: "I will personally ensure this is resolved."
- Offer immediate solution or escalate within [5 minutes]
- Follow up to confirm resolution

- Breakfast buffet setup complete by [6:00 AM]

- Dinner service ready by [5:30 PM]

**Server Standards:**

- Answer phone within [3 rings]

**Service Standards:**
- Be knowledgeable about:

- Create custom itineraries for guests staying [3+ nights]

**Communication:**
- Never say "I don't know" - say "Let me find out for you"
- Provide printed recommendations with maps
- Follow up: Call guest's room after activity: "How was [experience]?"

- Therapist greets guest by name

**Cleanliness:**

- Equipment: Cleaned and disinfected daily

## 4. SERVICE RECOVERY

### 4.1 Service Recovery Philosophy

**Principles:**
- Mistakes are opportunities to showcase our commitment
- Recover service immediately and generously
- Learn from every service failure to prevent recurrence

**LEARN Model:**
- **L**isten with empathy
- **E**mpathize and apologize
- **A**sk how to make it right (or offer solution)
- **R**esolve immediately
- **N**otify management and follow up

### 4.2 Common Issues and Responses

**Issue: Room Not Ready at Check-In**
- Apologize sincerely
- Offer:
  - Complimentary drink voucher for bar/lounge
  - Store luggage securely
  - Provide locker/shower access if available
  - Upgrade to next available room category (if possible)
  - Reduce rate by [X%] if delay exceeds [1 hour]
- Keep guest informed every [15 minutes] on status

**Issue: Noise Complaint**
- Respond within [5 minutes] of complaint
- Investigate and resolve (ask noisy guest to quiet down, Move complaining guest to quieter room)
- Offer: Complimentary breakfast, Room discount, Spa credit
- Follow up next morning: "Was the rest of your night peaceful?"

**Issue: Billing Error**
- Apologize immediately
- Correct error on the spot
- Offer small amenity as apology (e.g., Drink voucher)
- Waive incidental charges if error caused significant inconvenience

**Issue: Food Quality/Service Problem**
- Apologize sincerely
- Offer to remake dish or provide alternative
- Remove item from bill
- Offer complimentary dessert or drink
- Manager visits table to apologize personally

**Issue: Reservation Lost or Overbooked**
- **Never blame guest or another department**
- Options:
  1. "Walk" to comparable property (we pay, Arrange transportation, Bring back next day if multi-night)
  2. Upgrade to best available room (even suite) at originally booked rate
  3. Provide significant compensation (First night free, Major discount)
- GM personally calls guest to apologize

### 4.3 Empowerment Guidelines

**All Staff:**
- Empowered to offer [1 complimentary amenity, drink, or dessert] to apologize for minor issue

**Supervisors/Department Managers:**
- Empowered to waive charges up to $[Amount]
- Authorize room upgrades
- Offer [X] loyalty points or complimentary night

**General Manager:**
- Unlimited authority to resolve guest issues
- Philosophy: Resolve generously, Ensure guest leaves happy

## 5. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

### 5.1 Guest Communication

**Verbal Communication:**
- Professional yet warm tone
- Use "we" not "I" (emphasize team effort)
- Positive language:
  - Say: "I'll have that ready by 3 PM" NOT "I can't get that done until 3 PM"
  - Say: "Let me check on that for you" NOT "I don't know"
- Avoid jargon or acronyms

**Non-Verbal Communication:**
- Smile genuinely (reaches the eyes)
- Maintain eye contact
- Open body language (arms uncrossed)
- Nod to show understanding
- Appropriate personal space (60-90cm)

**Telephone Standards:**
- Answer within [3 rings]
- Greeting: "Good [time], [Department], this is [Name]. How may I assist you?"
- Never put guest on hold without permission: "May I place you on brief hold?"
- Return to call within [30 seconds] or offer callback
- End call: "Is there anything else I can assist you with? Thank you for calling, Mr./Ms. [Name]."

**Email Standards:**
- Respond within [2 hours during business hours, 24 hours otherwise]
- Professional format: Greeting, Body, Closing, Name and Title
- Proofread for errors
- Warm yet professional tone

### 5.2 Internal Communication

**Shift Handovers:**
- Logbook or digital handover system
- Key information: VIP arrivals, Guest issues, Maintenance problems, Special events

**Daily Briefings:**
- Department heads meet [daily at 9 AM]
- Share:
  - Occupancy and VIPs for the day
  - Special events or groups
  - Issues from previous day
  - Kudos and recognition

**Interdepartmental Communication:**
- Use designated communication channels (Radios, Internal chat system)
- Professional and respectful tone always
- Escalate conflicts to management promptly
